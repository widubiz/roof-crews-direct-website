FAQ

Straight answers

Who holds the license and the contract?

The roofing business. It is responsible for the contract, permits, payment, supervision, installation, warranty, and callbacks. Roof Crews Direct provides concierge coordination — generating interest, educating, gathering project information, and monitoring your journey from proposal to aftercare.

How does the markup work?

The roofing business gives its real cost; our markup is added on top, disclosed to both sides, and paid to DIRECT by the roofing business after the project is complete and paid. It is a markup — never a referral fee — and it is documented in the contractor agreement.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. The instant range estimate on this page is free and needs no contact information, and requesting a full proposal costs you nothing.

How fast is the full proposal?

24 to 48 hours after we have your project information — a complete presentation with measurements, findings, options, and pricing, not just a number.
